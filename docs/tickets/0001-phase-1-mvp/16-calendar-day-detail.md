# 16: Calendar day detail

**What to build:** Tapping a day in the calendar opens that day — every log recorded against every project, and an obvious way to add one where nothing was recorded.

**Blocked by:** 15

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Selecting a day lists all logs recorded on that business day across all projects, grouped by project.
- [ ] An empty day states plainly that nothing was recorded, and offers a direct way to record it.
- [ ] A log in the day view opens for editing, and edits are reflected when returning to the calendar.
- [ ] A rest day shows its rest status instead of an empty failure state.
- [ ] Day detail respects the business-day boundary: selecting a date shows that business day's records, not a midnight-to-midnight slice.
